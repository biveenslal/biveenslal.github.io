Focus Areas

Research Expertise

From fundamental quantum theory to deployable quantum hardware

🧭

Quantum Sensing & Magnetometry

I develop ultra-precise measurement systems leveraging quantum-enhanced techniques, focusing on magnetic field sensing, navigation, and atomic spectroscopy.

  • Optically Pumped Magnetometers (OPM) and alkali vapour cells
  • Shot-noise limited laser Doppler vibrometry
  • Magnetic field mapping for GPS-denied navigation
  • Noise budgeting and phase noise mitigation
  • High-sensitivity homodyne and heterodyne optical detection
🔬

Experimental Quantum Optics

I specialise in building state-of-the-art optical parametric systems, non-classical continuous-variable states, and robust cavity architectures.

  • Record-setting 12.6 dB squeezed light at 1550 nm for long-term duty cycle operation
  • Optical Parametric Amplifiers (OPAs) at 1550 nm and 1064 nm
  • Second harmonic generation (SHG) laser systems at 775 nm and 532 nm
  • Resonator bow-tie and monolithic cavity design & fabrication
  • Optomechanics: Brillouin optomechanics & single/multi-phonon subtraction
🔗

Quantum Information & Communications

My work investigates the protocols, channels, and resource bounds essential for global quantum networking and secure communications.

  • Continuous-variable quantum key distribution (CV-QKD) systems
  • Satellite-to-ground quantum communications across Low Earth Orbit (LEO)
  • Gaussian channel simulation via heralded non-unity gain teleportation
  • Noiseless linear amplification (NLA) for loss mitigation
  • Entropic accord and quantum state discrimination bounds

Precision Electronics & Control

Pioneering digital and analog servo loops for sub-Hz laser frequency locking and interferometer stabilization:

  • Pound-Drever-Hall (PDH) analog locking loops & demodulation
  • FPGA & Red-Pitaya based digital feedback control loops
  • Ultra-low noise, high-bandwidth balanced photoreceiver design
  • RF electronics and optical sideband resolved measurements
💻

Hardware Automation & Software

Full-stack software and hardware orchestration for continuous laboratory and deployment operation:

  • Real-time data processing stacks with Python, NumPy, SciPy & LabView
  • Automated sweep and telemetry systems replacing expensive benchtop units
  • Mathematical simulation of non-Gaussian quantum states with Mathematica & Julia
  • CAD modeling of low-noise optomechanics using Autodesk Fusion & FreeCAD

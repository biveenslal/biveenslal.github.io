About Biveen Shajilal

Physicist specialising in Quantum Sensing, Optics, and Metrology

Short Bio & Research Summary

I am a physicist currently serving as Senior Scientist in Quantum Sensing (Magnetometry) at Q-CTRL in Sydney, Australia. Prior to this, I was a Scientist in Quantum Physics at the A*STAR Quantum Innovation Centre (Q.InC) in Singapore, where I led research on squeezed light sources for quantum metrology and managed the development of the quantum metrology laboratory.

I received my PhD in Quantum Optics and Quantum Information from the Australian National University (ANU), working under Dr. Jiri Janousek, Dr. Syed M. Assad, Prof. Elanor Huntington, and Prof. Ping Koy Lam. My doctoral thesis investigated non-classical continuous-variable resources—squeezing, entanglement, and entropic accord—for secure quantum communication and precision metrology.

Alongside research, I provide strategic private consulting for quantum technology startups focusing on quantum key distribution (QKD) feasibility, hardware design, and noise budgeting.

Core Research Interests

Non-Classical Light Sources

Generation of high-purity squeezed light states at 1550 nm and 1064 nm for secure quantum communications and sub-shot-noise metrology.

Quantum Sensing & Magnetometry

Ultra-sensitive optically pumped magnetometers (OPMs), atomic vapour cells, and magnetic field mapping for navigation and biomedical imaging.

Quantum Metrology

Overcoming standard quantum limits, phase noise estimation, shot-noise-limited Doppler vibrometry, and state verification protocols.

Quantum Communications

Continuous-variable quantum key distribution (CV-QKD), satellite-to-ground quantum links, and noiseless linear amplification.

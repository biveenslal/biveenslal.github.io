Overview

Bridging quantum research and real-world technology

Exploring fundamental quantum resources and translating them into robust physical systems.

I am a physicist specialising in quantum sensing (magnetometry) at Q-CTRL. With a PhD in Quantum Optics from the Australian National University, I bridge the gap between cutting-edge quantum research and practical technological applications.

Currently, I serve as a Senior Scientist at Q-CTRL's Sensing Research Team based in Sydney, Australia, where I work on groundbreaking technology for quantum sensing and navigation. My work spans from fundamental quantum theory to the development of deployable, next-generation quantum hardware.
